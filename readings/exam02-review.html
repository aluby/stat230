3. Check necessary conditions for a multiple regression model and discuss the implications of a model deficiency

Formulas

I will give you the following formulas on the exam; you do not need to write them on your notecard. However, I will not answer any questions about notation or formulas during the exam.

Simple Linear Regression

  • \(s_x = \sqrt{\frac{\sum(X_i - \bar{X})^2}{n-1}}\)
  • \(r = \frac{1}{n} \sum (\frac{X_i - \bar{X}}{s_x})(\frac{Y_i - \bar{Y}}{s_y})\)
  • \(\hat \beta_0 = \bar{Y} - \hat\beta_1 \bar{X}\)
  • \(\hat\beta_1 = r \frac{s_y}{s_x}\)
  • \(SE(\hat \beta_0) = \hat{\sigma} \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} + \frac{\bar{X}^2}{\sum (X_i - \bar{X})^2}}\)
  • \(SE(\hat \beta_1) = \frac{\hat{\sigma}}{\sum (X_i - \bar{X})^2}\)
  • \(\hat{\sigma}=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(Y_{i}-\hat{Y}_{i})^{2}}{n-2}}\)
  • \(SD(e_{i})=\hat{\sigma}\sqrt{1-h_{i}}\)
  • \(SE(\hat{\mu}(Y|X))=\hat{\sigma}\sqrt{\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(X_{0}-\overline{X})^{2}}{\sum(X_{i}-\overline{X})^{2}}}\) -\(SE(\hat{Y})=\hat{\sigma}\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{n}+\frac{(X_{0}-\overline{X})^{2}}{\sum(X_{i}-\overline{X})^{2}}}\)

Multiple Linear Regression

  • \(\hat{\gamma}=c_{1}\hat{\beta}_{i}+c_{2}\hat{\beta}_{j}\)
  • \(Var(\hat{\gamma})=c_{1}^{2}Var(\hat{\beta}_{i})+c_{2}^{2}Var(\hat{\beta}_{j})+2c_{1}c_{2}Cov(\hat{\beta}_{i},\hat{\beta}_{j})\)
  • \(R^{2}=\frac{SSReg}{SSTotal}=1-\frac{SSE}{SSTotal}\)
  • \(R_{adj}^{2}=\frac{SSTotal/(n-1)-SSE/(n-(p+1))}{SSTotal/(n-1)} = \frac{MST-MSE}{MST}\)
  • \(F=\frac{(SSE(reduced)-SSE(full))/(df_{reduced}-df_{full})}{SSE(full)/df_{full}}\)
  • \(F = \frac{MSReg}{MSE}\)
  • \(e_{i}=Y_{i}-\hat{Y_{i}}\)
  • \(r_{i}=\frac{\hat{e}_{i}}{\hat{\sigma}\sqrt{1-h_{i}}}\)
  • \(h_{i}=\frac{1}{n-1}\left[\frac{X_{i}-\overline{X}}{s_{X}}\right]^{2}+\frac{1}{n} = \frac{1}{n}+\frac{{(X_{i}-\overline{X})}^{2}}{\sum(X_{i}-\overline{X})^{2}}\)
  • \(\sum_{i}h_{i}=p+1\)
  • \(D_{i}=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{(\hat{Y}_{j(i)}-\hat{Y}_{j})^{2}}{(p+1)\hat{\sigma}} = \frac{r_{i}^{2}}{p+1}\left(\frac{h_{i}}{1-h_{i}}\right)\)
  • \(VIF_i=1/(1-R_{i}^{2})\)
